I am currently pursuing a PhD at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). I received my BEng in Automation from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen (HITSZ), China.

My research focuses on robotics, robot learning, embodied AI, and vision-language-action (VLA) models. I am interested in building intelligent robots that can perceive, reason, and act in the real world.
